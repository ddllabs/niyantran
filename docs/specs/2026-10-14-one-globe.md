# Spec: one globe across the marketing site (F141)

> **Status: Living.** Owner request 2026-10-14: the client's globe, now on the homepage (F140), should
> replace the old globe wherever it appears. Owner accepted the recommendations: the globe only (the
> sign-in, sign-up and pricing pages keep their current page styling), then retire the old globe.
> Findings below were read from the code on 2026-10-14.

## Current state

- **New globe (F140):** `<nter-data-globe>` from `src/marketing/nterGlobe.js` and `nterGlobe.css`, on the
  homepage only. Seven data labels on two inclined orbits pass behind Earth; a label opens its desk or
  module through `openGlobeDestination` (`src/marketing/globeDestinations.js`): signed out, it
  remembers the destination in `niyantranLand` / `niyantranFeature` and goes to sign-in.
- **Old globe:** `AuthEarth` (`src/marketing/AuthEarth.jsx`, scene `authEarthScene.js`, motion
  `earthMotion.js`), on three pages:

  | Page | Use | Wrapper |
  |---|---|---|
  | Sign in (`LoginPage.jsx:134`) | `<AuthEarth />`: six persona chips; hovering one shows what that persona gets | `.mkt-login-earth-visual` (absolute on desktop; 300 px block on narrow screens) |
  | Sign up (`SignupPage.jsx:165`) | `<AuthEarth persona onPersona>`: the chips mirror the form's "Working as" radio group (`SignupPage.jsx:278`), which is the real picker and is required before submit | `.mkt-signup-illustration` (down to 90 px and 64 px wide on small phones) |
  | Pricing (`PricingPage.jsx:107`) | `<AuthEarth labels={false} />`, no labels | `.mkt-pr-earth` (hidden below 1024 px) |

- Both globes use the same three texture files in `/brand/auth-earth/` (byte-identical, checked in
  F140) and the same CC BY 4.0 credit in the footer.

## Outcome

1. **Sign in, sign up and pricing show the new globe** with its seven data labels, orbits, occlusion
   and pausing, sized to each page's existing wrapper. The pages' own layout, colours and copy stay.
2. **Labels behave as on the homepage.** On sign-in and sign-up a label click stores the destination
   and stays on (or opens) sign-in, so the visitor lands on that desk or module once signed in. On
   pricing, the same. One shared React wrapper (`DataGlobe.jsx`) loads the module after first paint,
   wires `data-select` to `openGlobeDestination`, and is used by all four pages, replacing the inline
   wiring in `HomePage.jsx`.
3. **Sign-up keeps its persona picker in the form.** The globe no longer mirrors it; the "Working as"
   radio group is unchanged and still required.
4. **Small wrappers hide the labels.** Where the globe is drawn smaller than about 240 px (sign-up's
   90 px and 64 px thumbnails, sign-in's narrow-screen block if it falls below that), only the Earth
   and rings show; labels are hidden by the page's wrapper CSS, not by changing the component.
5. **The old globe is retired**: `AuthEarth.jsx`, `authEarthScene.js`, `earthMotion.js` and their
   tests, and the CSS only they use (`.auth-earth*`, the persona-chip `.mkt-pchip*` rules), after a
   search proves no other importer. The footer credit and `/brand/auth-earth/` files stay (the new
   globe uses them).

## Acceptance

- Tests: each of the three pages renders `<nter-data-globe>` through `DataGlobe` and no longer
  `AuthEarth`; `DataGlobe` wires a label to `openGlobeDestination` (signed out: stores the
  destination and asks to sign in); sign-up still requires a persona from the form; no file imports
  the retired modules. `publicPlans.test.js` (which pins `<AuthEarth labels={false} />` on pricing) is
  updated to the new globe. Each guard fails without its code.
- Browser at 1440, 1024, 939 and 375 px on all three pages: the globe sits where the old one did,
  labels pass behind Earth, a label click on sign-in remembers the destination, sign-up's small
  thumbnail shows no labels, no horizontal scroll.
- `npm run lint`, `npm test`, `npm run build`.
