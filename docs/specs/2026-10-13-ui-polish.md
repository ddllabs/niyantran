> **Status: Living.** Owner request 2026-10-13, on `task/ui-polish` (its own worktree, in parallel
> with the AI-credits work on another branch). Findings were read from the code and measured on
> localhost at 1440, 1920 and 375 px on 2026-10-13.

## F133. Desk tabs: separator back, less space (owner decisions)

**Now.** Since `38fae22` (2026-10-08) the desk tabs sit inside the header with `border:0`, so the
line between the search row and the tabs is gone; the header's only line is under the tabs. F112
(`dbb5cde`) made the tab row 52 px plus 6 px padding around a 40 px pill. Measured at 1440 px: the
header is 119 px (utilities 12–52, tabs 60–118, line at 119). nter.pro, the reference, has a line
under the search row and a 42 px tab row (about 92 px in all). Above 1800 px our tabs share the logo
row.

**Outcome.**
- A full-width 1 px line (`var(--line)`) between the search row and the tabs, at every width.
- The tabs are their own row at every width, including above 1800 px.
- The highlight and its underline stay (owner: "keep the pill, just tighten"); the row is 42 px.
  Owner follow-up: no space around the tabs, so each tab and the current desk's highlight fill the
  row from the line above to the line below (square corners, the underline on the bottom line).
- Hot topics stays directly under the header's bottom line.

**Acceptance.** Browser measurements at 1920, 1440, 939 and 375 px: a separator above the tabs
spanning the header width; tab row about 42 px; no horizontal scroll at 375 px except the tab strip's
own; the 900 px icon rule unchanged.

## F134. Header search results can be dragged to AI research

**Now.** Neither the Catalogue results (`DeskCatalogueSearch.jsx`) nor the Records results
(`CommandSearch.jsx`) use the shared drag helper (`src/lib/aiDrop.js`). Record results drop the row
the search RPC returns (`commandSearch.js`), the record button cancels mousedown (which also stops a
native drag), and the search is a modal `<dialog>`, so nothing behind it can take a drop.

**Outcome.**
- A Records result that is a record drags as a `row` payload (`rowDragProps`), with the row the RPC
  returned; a module result drags as a `feature` payload; a desk result as a `tab` payload.
- A Catalogue result drags as a `feature` payload.
- On drag start the search window gets out of the way without unmounting the dragged element, and AI
  research opens (`openAiResearch()`), so the item can be dropped into the chat on any page,
  including Home. The window closes when the drag ends.
- Clicking and keyboard use are unchanged.

**Acceptance.** Unit tests: the record result keeps its row; each result kind carries the right
payload; a drag start opens AI and hides the window. Browser: drag a Records result and a Catalogue
result into the chat on Home and on a module page; the chip appears.

## F135. The side panel collapses on an outside click or Escape on Desk and Record too

**Now.** The only outside-click handler (`SidePanel.jsx`) runs while the panel shows AI. Docked Desk
and Record have no outside-click or Escape handling, so the panel "closes on some pages and not
others": pages without a rail can only show AI. The side-panel plan
(`docs/plans/2026-10-03-side-panel.md`) said Desk and Record collapse on Esc or a click outside.

**Outcome (owner decision: collapse to the slim bar).**
- On a rail page, with the panel open on Desk or Record and not expanded, a completed click outside
  it collapses it to the slim bar (`collapse`); the selection is kept, so the desks that auto-select
  a row do not reopen it.
- Clicking another row still switches the record: the collapse runs in the capture phase and the
  row's selection then reopens the panel on Record.
- Escape does the same on Desk and Record.
- Found in the browser check: after a collapse, clicking the selected row again deselected it (the
  tables' second-click toggle), so its record could not be reopened. Now, with the panel collapsed,
  picking the selected row again reopens its record (`pickIntent`). The desks that deselect on a
  second click name the row (`onSelect(null, { toggled: row })`); a clear with no row named (a
  feature or filter change) still clears.
- Not outside: the panel itself, an open native `<dialog>`, an `aria-modal` dialog, and the existing
  keep marks. Expanded mode keeps its current rule (the first outside click restores the width).
  AI keeps its current rules.

**Acceptance.** Tests: outside click collapses on Record and Desk; not while expanded, not inside a
`<dialog open>`, not on AI (AI keeps `dismiss-ai`); a row click after the collapse reopens Record;
Escape collapses. Each guard is shown to fail without its code. Browser check on a table module
and on Alliances.
