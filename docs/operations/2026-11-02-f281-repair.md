# F281 repair: 97 broken corpus documents

> **Status: Historical (dated 2026-11-02).** Record of the F281 repair as executed on 2026-11-02 with
> `scripts/f281-repair/repair.mjs`. It states what ran and what the checks showed. It does not authorise
> further writes. Open items are tracked in [open work](../plans/open-work.md) under F281, F275 and F278.

## Summary

Ninety-seven documents in the Bills and Regulatory Body Watch features held text that was another bill's,
shifted or damaged, or carried a stray cover page, or had a title that did not match the text. On 2026-11-02
all 97 were repaired in NTER in five batches (20, 20, 20, 20, 17). Each batch was read back by the tool and
audited by the supervisor with read-only SQL on NTER. The indexed document count is unchanged at 17,606.
Chunks rose from 392,945 to 395,375, and every chunk has an embedding.

The repair covers the 97 documents in the manifest's batch. The 133 documents that the manifest excludes, with
reasons, remain open (see [What remains](#what-remains)).

## Why

The two reports are [the broken-documents investigation](../research/2026-11-01-f281-broken-documents.md),
which found eleven broken documents and the replace route, and
[the triage](../research/2026-11-01-f281-triage.md), which checked 264 Bill documents flagged by a title test
(43 genuine mismatches, 9 garbage OCR, 3 held on purpose, 1 unclear and 208 false alarms) and 45 Regulatory
documents with partial text.

Repairs by action, from `scripts/f281-repair/manifest.json`:

| Action | Documents | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `ocr` | 51 | Mistral OCR of the official PDF, replacing scanned or garbage text |
| `rekey` | 31 | Text unchanged; new title and `document_key` (the text is another bill) |
| `retitle_unlink` | 10 | Text unchanged; new title, desk link removed (`file_url_ambiguous` true) |
| `decode` | 2 | Glyph-ID text layer decoded per font (+29) from the official PDF |
| `strip_cover` | 2 | Another bill's cover page removed from the stored text |
| `reextract` | 1 | Plain re-extraction from a text-bearing official PDF, no OCR |

Every Regulatory repair reads the official PDF, never the client pack text, because the pack text is truncated.

The manifest was filled with the full document ids (97 of 97) and six source PDFs were pinned before `build`.
Filling ids or pinning a PDF changes the manifest, so `build` and `dry-run` were run again before `write`.

### Review before the write

- An independent review of the tool (commit `6866dab4`) found three blockers and seven major issues. All ten
  were fixed before the manifest was executed (`ae6782bc`).
- `3d51552f` (the synopsis of the Indian Divorce (Amendment) Bill 2001, stored under `bill:2000:76`) is
  allowed without a desk record, because none exists for `bill:2000:LXXVI` and the as-introduced document
  `af83bcf2` already holds that key.
- The supervisor read the 46 documents marked LOOK:
  - every CCI order's printed case number matched its file name (34 by pattern, 3 by their named forms, 2 by
    reading);
  - the TRAI documents' openings matched their titles;
  - the six garbage-OCR Bills are complete short bills whose old text was OCR noise;
  - the 41 re-key and unlink titles matched the triage and the desk records.
- The write is bound to the reviewed content: `--confirm` is the SHA-256 of `build/review.json`,
  `d5bf13e84293181fe6abc1c43161cb23c76fb397d5caeac377cb48a427103241`.

## Write, batch by batch

Each batch was posted, read back by the tool (all OK), and audited by the supervisor. The audit ran read-only
SQL on NTER: the documents were indexed; `chunker_version` was 2; every chunk was embedded; `content_sha256`
equalled the SHA-256 of the stored text; re-keyed documents had their desk records; unlinked documents had no
`document_key` and `file_url_ambiguous` true. After each batch `ANALYZE` was run on `document_chunks`,
`documents` and `desk_rows`. The authorization text was recorded with every batch in the tool's work directory
(`write/authorization.json`) and in `review.md`.

| Batch | Documents | Readback | Supervisor audit | ANALYZE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 | OK | Passed | Run |
| 2 | 20 | OK | Passed | Run |
| 3 | 20 | OK | Passed | Run |

Across the five batches the writes inserted 4,459 chunks and deleted 2,029 (net +2,430, which matches the
change in the total chunk count below). Two orderings were required by the manifest and held:
`3d51552f` before `4530253a`, and `648594ab` before `0cfda1b0`.

## Result

- 97 documents carry `metadata.text_repair`.
- Indexed documents: 17,606, unchanged.
- Chunks: 392,945 before, 395,375 after. None lacks an embedding.
- No two indexed documents share a `content_sha256`.

## Rollback position

- Nothing has been rolled back.
- `rollback-build` builds restore payloads from the pre-write snapshots. An unlinked document is re-linked
  (`file_url_ambiguous` false, with its key fields restored). The snapshots and the rollback review are kept in
  the tool's work directory, `~/.niyantran/f281-repair`, outside the repository.
- `dry-run`, `write` and `readback` accept `--rollback`. A rollback therefore runs through the same write step
  and its gates.

## Consequence for the retrieval eval

- The local replica (`niyantran_retrieval_replica`) is a 2026-11-01 snapshot. For these 97 documents it no longer
  equals NTER.
- The v2 baseline ([report](../research/2026-11-01-retrieval-baseline-v2.md)) and the Phase B rehearsals run on
  that snapshot, and are unaffected.
- A run of the eval against live NTER would now fail its fingerprint check for any question whose gold chunk
  is in a repaired document. Refresh the replica, or rebuild the question set, before a live comparison.

  Ask the client for these files in the first-collection summary, not in a separate message.

- **Held on purpose.** `0384f9b5`, `1b801b87` and `624ae9cb` (`mapping_hold`) stay held until the key
  collision is resolved.
- **Key-less false alarms.** 108 documents with no key and a file name as the title. Re-titling is optional and
  was not part of this batch. Also `85377b0f`, `b77caf5d` and `17bb03c1` (small defects, not requested).
- **Trivial.** `410ebf75` and `9726bbf4`: stray glyphs in one chunk, not worth a re-ingest on their own.
- **Suspected stray covers, not yet read.** Flagged by the title guard: `3cbeef80`, `548fd359`, `6fffba70`,
  `70b72057`, `9c83ba9d`, `ea463d40`. Not flagged: `04cd2c4b`, `19e8dd76`, `af83bcf2`, `c2a56a17`, `ecf06955`.
  Add each as `strip_cover` only after reading its opening.
- **Duplicate.** `8bffca18` and `ee462209` now both carry the title The Finance Act, 2003 and are unlinked. They
  are the same file (504 chunks each). Decide which to keep.
- **Bill key collision.** `bill:2012:74` names two different bills, and the desk record under `bill:2011:116`
  names a 2010 bill. Bills adapter (F275).
- **Lessons for the bulk pipeline (F278).** The client packs carry truncated OCR text for scanned documents.
  Bulk acceptance must test text per page and OCR thin scans from the official source. The title guard
  (`src/lib/titleGuard.js`) and the bill-number check belong in the bulk pipeline's acceptance checks.
