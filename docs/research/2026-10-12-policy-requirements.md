# Website policy requirements for Razorpay activation (India)

> **Status: Historical (dated 2026-10-12).** Desk research for F107. Not legal
> advice. [V] = read in the official text; [R] = Razorpay's own docs/blog;
> [S] = secondary source or uncertain.

## Razorpay

- [R] Live keys need linked pages: About us, Contact us, Pricing, Terms and
  conditions, Privacy policy, Cancellation/Refund policy; plus a sample
  invoice. If payment needs a login, Razorpay needs reviewer test credentials.
  An automated check reviews the pages. Docs: "Business Website Details".
- [R] Show the registered legal entity name (matching PAN/bank), a physical
  address (no PO box), phone, email, business hours; footer links to Terms
  and Privacy on every page; refund policy with timelines; INR prices with no
  hidden fees; grievance officer name, designation, email, phone.
  Blog "Payment Gateway Compliance in 2026" (6 Apr 2026).
- [S] A Shipping/Delivery page is no longer listed for India but is safe to
  publish: nothing physical shipped; access granted electronically on payment
  confirmation, with a timeline.
- [R/S] GST is not a universal onboarding prerequisite ("I don't have GST"
  option); whether a Pvt Ltd may use it is uncertain: confirm with Razorpay.
- [S] RBI e-mandate framework (21 Apr 2026): AFA to register/modify/cancel and
  for the first debit; later debits up to ₹15,000 without AFA; pre-debit
  notice at least 24 h ahead. The site should state auto-renewal, cycle,
  amount and how to cancel. CCPA dark-pattern guidelines (2023) ban
  subscription traps and drip pricing.

## DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025

- [V] Rules G.S.R. 846(E), 13/14 Nov 2025: Consent Manager rule from about
  Nov 2026; notice, security, breach, retention, contact, children and rights
  rules from about **13–14 May 2027**. No notified amendment shortening this
  was found.
- [V] Notice (Rule 3): standalone, plain language, itemised data, specific
  purposes, how to withdraw consent, exercise rights and complain to the
  Board. Rule 6 security incl. one-year log retention; Rule 7 breach notice to
  users and the Board (detailed report within 72 h); Rule 8 keep personal
  data/logs at least one year; Rule 9 publish a privacy contact; Rule 10
  verifiable parental consent under 18; Rule 14 publish how to make requests
  and a response period of at most 90 days; nomination right.

## Applies today

- [S] IT Act s.43A + SPDI Rules 2011 (until DPDP's 18-month mark): privacy
  policy on the site (practices, data types, purposes, disclosures, security);
  consent and purpose awareness; named Grievance Officer; grievances resolved
  within one month.
- [S] Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 likely apply to a SaaS
  selling its own plans: legal name, address, customer care and grievance
  officer (acknowledge in 48 h, resolve in one month); refund/cancellation
  terms; how to cancel recurring payments; total price with tax breakdown; no
  pre-ticked consent.
- [S] IT Intermediary Rules 2021 (and Feb 2026 amendment) only if the service
  hosts users' content (future file storage): lawyer to confirm.

## GST

- [S] Services threshold ₹20 lakh aggregate turnover per PAN (West Bengal),
  including the company's other income: CA to confirm. An unregistered person
  must not collect any amount as tax (CGST s.32(1)): show final prices
  (₹399 / ₹999 / ₹9,999) with no GST line; plain invoices without GSTIN.

## Needs a lawyer or CA

once storage ships; scope of e-commerce rules for professional plans; the
no-refund policy (Razorpay and consumer law push back); 18+ restriction; AI
output liability and cross-border processing disclosures.
