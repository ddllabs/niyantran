# National v1.1 to v1.3: loads, then close-out

> **Status: Living (2026-11-19).** Orders the work that loads what is left in packs v1.1, v1.2 and v1.3, and lists
> what must be true to close the hybrid search programme and the corpus ingestion programme. It narrows the
> [readiness plan](2026-11-18-national-scale-readiness.md) to its next steps and does not replace it. Spec:
> [bulk ingestion](../specs/2026-11-01-bulk-ingestion.md); scope: [ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-national-v1.1-ingestion-scope.md).
> The only list of open work is [open-work.md](open-work.md). Nothing here changes production without the developer's
> specific yes for that step, after a rolled-back dry run.

## Problem

Ingestion cannot start on these entries because of routes and data gaps, not capacity:

1. Five desks have no admission route (F367).
2. Search-only admission writes no link from a table row to its document, so the row still reads as "no document"
   and a chat on that row goes to web search although the text is in the corpus (F368).
3. The Regulatory screen accepts PDF text only; most of v1.3 is web-page text (F369). The desk is also held until its
   search is fixed (F355).
4. The runner cannot replace text (F357), so v1.2's 1,796 corrections wait.
5. The pack reader refuses a valid file written in two compressed parts (F370).
6. The packs spell two desk names differently from the catalogue (F371).
7. Corrigenda have no file link, no mapping and a type name the Bills screen does not know (F372).
8. Row files and page boxes have no loader; Cabinet has 6 rows on NTER for 868 documents (F373).

## Close-out conditions

**Hybrid search programme** closes when:

- NCLT hybrid has run a month with the fallback recorded as fired or not (F288), and keep-warm stays applied (F324);
- Regulatory search passes its spec or the developer accepts the result on record (F355);
- each of F284, F286, F292, F296 and F298 is fixed or closed with a recorded reason;
- chunk headers stay decided-later (F289), on record.

**Corpus ingestion programme** closes when:

- every servable National entry in v1.1 to v1.6 is loaded or has a recorded wait (F278, F358);
- the capacity option is in place and measured on NTER (F279);
- replacements, row links and partitions have their routes (F357, F368, F285);
- the runner survives the operator's machine sleeping (F356), and F301, F305, F293 and F302 are fixed or closed;
- the Bills remainder (F311, F312, F314, F325, F333, F334, F336) and the Regulatory remainder (F337, F338, F346) are
  fixed or closed;
- the migration history question is answered (F283).
