### T2. The pool's part lifetime
- **Acceptance:**
  - a job's own failure (a page or thumbnail) fails that job only;
  - a part is closed when its transport or document fails;
  - `cleanup()` is called after each draw;
  - tests on fakes, failing first.
- **Verify:** `npx vitest run src/ai/page-viewer/pdfPool.test.js`.

**T2, recorded 2026-10-02.**
- **The part fails itself:** a part is marked failed when its range reads fail, and it is closed
  only then, or when its document fails to open. A page or thumbnail that fails alone fails only
  its own job.
- `page.cleanup()` runs after every job. pdf.js holds it while another render of the page runs.
- Two tests (a thumbnail failing beside a page on the same part, and cleanup after a draw) failed
  first and pass now. All 22 pool tests pass.

### T4. Keeping the reader's place without `overflow-anchor`
- **Acceptance:**
  - `TextDocument` records the anchor (the page under the top of the view, and the offset into it)
    on scroll, and restores it before paint when texts arrive, only where
    `CSS.supports('overflow-anchor', 'auto')` is false;
  - a hooks test with a fake region;
  - a harness check with `overflow-anchor: none`.

**T4, recorded 2026-10-02.**
- **The arithmetic** is pure and tested in `textModel.js`:
  - `pageAt`, the page under a line;
  - `textAnchor`, the page at the top of the view and the offset into it;
  - `anchoredTop`, the scroll that restores the anchor;
  - `needsManualAnchoring`.

  This replaces the planned fake-region hooks test.
- **`TextDocument`:**
  - records the anchor after every scroll, its own included;
  - restores it before paint whenever texts or the cited pieces change;
  - does this only where `CSS.supports('overflow-anchor', 'auto')` is false;
  - takes its current page from `pageAt` too.
- **On the harness:** the 1,000-page document with Chrome's anchoring forced off, page 491's heading
  at the top, and pages 481–490 loading above it.
  - Without the fix (`?anchor=none`), the heading was pushed 455 px down.
  - With it (`?anchor=manual`), it stayed at 0 px.
- **Checks:** lint and 388 viewer tests pass.
- **Not checked in Safari itself:** its absence of `overflow-anchor` is the reason for the change. An
  owner check on production remains.
