↓ HTTPS

Does

  • Saves the document, hidden until finished
  • Chunks: ~1,000 chars, 200 overlap, headings and tables kept whole (chunker v2)
  • Embeds only new chunks: OpenRouter text-embedding-3-small, 1,536 numbers
  • Commits 100 chunks at a time, then marks the document searchable
  • 2,379 embedding calls, 18.1M tokens, $0.36 in total

Gaps the new pipeline has to close

01

No OCR

Every text came pre-made from NTER-Ingest. A new scanned PDF cannot enter NTER today.

02

No upload path

Only a person running a script on the laptop can add documents. No user or scheduled route.

03

No page numbers

0 of 54,219 chunks. Pages were merged at OCR, so citations can't point to a page.

04

Empty chunk metadata

By design; everything sits on the document. No chunk-level filter or provenance.

05

Low coverage

2,338 of 15,093 OCR documents; 0 of 18,071 PDF-text documents; dataset records only via desk rows.

06

Links mostly bills

File-name matching gives regulators and questions almost no source URL.

07

Uneven metadata

2,335 documents carry 12 fields, 3 carry the full saved record.

08

Upstream work unused

NTER-Ingest's chunks, licence classes and held flags are ignored or stored unused.
